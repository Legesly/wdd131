Ghana Travel Guide – Site Plan

This document is the planning blueprint for the Ghana Travel Guide website project.

Site Name

Ghana Travel Guide

Ghana Travel Guide is the site name. It clearly tells visitors that the website helps people explore Ghana, including culture, food, destinations, and travel tips.

Optional domain: ghanatravelguide.org

Site Purpose

The site will provide reliable, easy-to-understand information for people planning a trip to Ghana. Visitors will find destination highlights, cultural information, food recommendations, and travel tips. A contact page will let users ask questions or request help. The finished site will have at least three pages with consistent navigation: Home, About Ghana, and Contact.

Scenarios

These questions represent the target audience: first-time travelers and people curious about Ghana.

  • What are the top tourist attractions to visit in Ghana?
  • Where can I find authentic Ghanaian food when I visit?
  • How do I contact someone for travel questions or local guidance?

Typography

Two Google Fonts will be used on the site plan and the finished website.

Montserrat – used for headings (h1, h2, h3) and the logo name.

Open Sans – used for paragraphs, lists, and general body text.
