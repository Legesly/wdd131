Before you go

  • English is the official language, and it is widely used in cities and with tour guides.
  • The currency is the Ghana cedi. Carry some cash for markets and smaller chop bars.
  • Cape Coast and Kakum pair well in two days. Kumasi and Mole need more travel time.
  • Ask permission before photographing people, ceremonies, or inside palaces.
